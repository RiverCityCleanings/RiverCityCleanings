FAQ

Common questions.

Do you bring your own supplies?
Yes — we bring everything we need. Portable vacuum, extension cords, cleaning supplies. The only thing we need from you is access to an electrical outlet so we can power our equipment.
Do I need to provide electricity?
We don't ask you to provide any supplies, but we do need access to a working electrical outlet at your location. We bring our own extension cords so we can usually reach wherever we need to work — just let us know if you have any concerns about that.
Do you pressure wash?
No — we don't offer pressure washing. We do standard cleaning like sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, and surface wiping. We also don't do professional detailing or deep restoration cleaning. If you're not sure whether we can handle what you need, just text us and ask.
Why are prices different for different jobs?
A few things affect the price: how far away you are (we travel by bike with our gear on a wagon), how big the space is, and how much work it actually takes when we get there. We always give you an estimate before we start — so there are no surprises. If it ends up being easier or harder than expected, we'll talk with you about it first.
Do you mow lawns?
Possibly — we're still working on offering lawn mowing consistently. We do light yard work for sure (raking, cleaning up the yard, etc.). For mowing specifically, just text us and ask. We'll let you know if it's something we can do for your situation.
How do you get to jobs?
We use bikes and pull our equipment on a wagon. It works great for most jobs in and around Wisconsin Rapids. That's also why travel distance is a factor in our pricing — longer trips take more time and effort on our end. We're always upfront about that when we give you a quote.
What if I'm not happy with the job?
We don't offer refunds — but we always try to make things right. If something wasn't done to your satisfaction, let us know right away and we'll do our best to address it. We're not trying to rip anyone off. We want you to be genuinely happy with the work we did.
Are you insured or licensed?
We're a young startup cleaning business. We're not currently licensed or insured in a formal business sense — we're upfront about that. We take care working in your space and will always treat your property with respect. If that's a concern for you, we totally understand and respect your decision.
